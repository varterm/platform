The **Chrome** build of **Varterm TTS** can do more than a selection or a full page.

- **Video transcripts.** On a video with captions, open **Read Video Transcript**. Search the whole transcript, click a line to hear from there, or click a timestamp to jump the video.
- **Jump and speed.** Back and ahead from the popup or the reader panel. Speed chips run from 0.75× to 2×; changing speed re-reads the current part.
- **Optional chat auto-read.** Hear new assistant replies as they finish on the AI chats you grant. Off until you turn a site on. Chrome asks then, not at install. Existing replies stay quiet. Revoking Site access turns that site off.
- **Read shortcut.** Select text and press `Alt+Shift+R`. Chrome uses Ctrl+Shift+R to reload the page.

Install from the [extensions hub](/extensions#chrome) or the [install guide](/install-extensions). The same voice engine as the [web reader](/) still strips markdown so headings and code fences do not get spoken as punctuation.
