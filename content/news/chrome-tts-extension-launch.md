The **Chrome** build of **Varterm TTS** is out for installs from our [extensions hub](/extensions). Highlights:

- **Selection or full-page** listening with a predictable UI.
- Fits **markdown-heavy** layouts and noisy DOMs typical of forums and docs.
- Works beside the **same Edge-voice backbone** many of you already use on the web app.

Pair it with **[Cursor](/extensions#editors) or [VS Code](/extensions#editors)** when work moves from editor to browser, or keep everything in Chromium if that is where you hang out day to day.

We will iterate on manifests, permissions, and store listing status as reviewers catch up — check **[/extensions](/extensions)** whenever you need binaries or unpacked testing tips.
