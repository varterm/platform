# Varterm Marketing Strategy (Canonical)

Last updated: 2026-05-24

## Core positioning

Varterm is a free, privacy-conscious, long-form text-to-speech platform that works across web, editor, and browser workflows, with better handling of messy modern text.

## Messaging hierarchy (in order)

1. **Free and privacy-conscious by default**
   - No login, no paywall.
   - Local/offline voice options for privacy-focused usage.

2. **Long-form first**
   - Built for articles, books, docs, transcripts, and AI output.
   - Reliable chunked playback for large content.

3. **Clean speech from messy text**
   - Markdown cleanup.
   - Special-character and formatting-aware listening improvements.

4. **Cross-platform consistency**
   - Web + Cursor + VS Code + Chrome.
   - Shared listening workflow across contexts.

5. **Listener experience quality**
   - Natural voices, pacing control, progress visibility, and session continuity.

## Mission statement

Varterm exists to make long-form text listenable everywhere, with privacy-conscious workflows and clean speech from modern, formatting-heavy content.

## Primary audiences

1. **Hackers/developers**
   - Jobs: listen to docs, AI output, and technical text quickly and privately.
   - Proof points: no-login flow, offline/local options, editor integrations.

2. **Heavy readers/knowledge workers**
   - Jobs: convert long reading queues into listenable sessions.
   - Proof points: long-form chunking, progress tracking, reliable playback.

3. **Mission-driven institutions (archives/libraries/publishers)**
   - Jobs: improve accessibility and listening fidelity for long texts.
   - Proof points: long-form quality, text cleanup, cross-platform access.

## GTM priorities

1. **SEO intent capture**
   - Maintain and expand long-tail pages around long-form, markdown, privacy, and platform-specific terms.

2. **Platform distribution**
   - Convert web users to extension installs and extension users back to web utility.

3. **Partner outreach**
   - Outreach to mission-aligned institutions where long-form accessibility matters.

4. **Narrative cadence**
   - Continue markdown-based news/changelog updates to support trust and discoverability.

## SEO priorities

1. Keep technical SEO clean (metadata, schema, sitemap, robots, canonical rules).
2. Expand content around:
   - Privacy-focused TTS workflows.
   - Special-character cleanup for natural speech.
   - Long-form accessibility use cases.
3. Reduce over-reliance on ChatGPT-specific traffic terms by broadening problem-based intent coverage.

## Messaging guardrails

- Do not frame privacy claims beyond what the product and policy support.
- Avoid leading with only “free tool” language; pair with mission and quality.
- Keep copy concrete with proof points (chunking, controls, platforms, cleanup).
- Preserve a builder-friendly tone for developer audiences.
