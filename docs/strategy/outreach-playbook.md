# Outreach Playbook

Last updated: 2026-05-24

## Outreach objective

Start conversations with mission-aligned teams that care about long-form accessibility and high-fidelity listening (archives, libraries, publishers, educational repositories).

## Core outreach message

Varterm helps audiences listen to long and messy text with better fidelity by combining long-form TTS, cleanup for formatting-heavy content, and privacy-conscious workflows across web, editor, and browser surfaces.

## Message blocks to reuse

### Value blocks

- **Long-form reliability**: handles long passages with chunked playback and continuity.
- **Text cleanup quality**: reduces markdown/syntax noise for cleaner listening.
- **Privacy-conscious options**: no-login flow and local/offline-compatible usage modes.
- **Cross-platform access**: web, Cursor, VS Code, and Chrome.

### Mission alignment blocks

- “Your content has lasting value and should be accessible in listening format.”
- “We care about preserving context and readability in long-form audio.”
- “We want to support your mission with lightweight, practical integration.”

## Audience-specific framing

### Archives/libraries
- Lead with preservation + accessibility.
- Mention long historical texts and complex formatting challenges.

### Publishers/editorial teams
- Lead with reader experience and content consumption flexibility.
- Mention long-form article listening and retention.

### Developer platforms/docs teams
- Lead with docs readability and AI/markdown-heavy output cleanup.
- Mention editor/browser workflows and developer ergonomics.

## Example outreach blurb (short)

Greetings and thank you for the work your team does.

I have been reading your long-form materials and wanted to explore whether we could help improve listening accessibility. I run [Varterm](https://varterm.com), a free, privacy-conscious long-form TTS platform that handles markdown/special-character-heavy content and improves listening flow across web, editor, and browser workflows.

If useful, I would love to discuss a lightweight collaboration to help your audience listen to long texts in higher fidelity.

## Email skeleton (long)

Subject: Exploring long-form listening support for your readers

1. Open with appreciation for their mission/content.
2. Mention the specific content/workflow where listening is hard today.
3. Explain Varterm in one sentence (free, privacy-conscious, long-form, cleanup quality).
4. Offer practical next step (quick call, pilot, or sample integration).
5. Close with mission alignment and gratitude.

## Outreach guardrails

- Be mission-first, not transactional.
- Avoid overclaiming privacy or integration readiness.
- Keep asks lightweight and specific.
- Personalize every message with concrete references to their content.
